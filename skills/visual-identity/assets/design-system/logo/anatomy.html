Anatomy and clear space

The mark is a signature, not an explanation. It does not carry meaning — it collects it.

Structure

SymbolThe ember, reduced to a folded geometric form. Not a picture of one.
WordmarkDrawn twice — Latin and Arabic — locking to the same symbol. Never one translated from the other.
LockupVertical is primary. Do not alter the relationship between the parts.
Bilingual means two drawings, not a translation. The scripts have different rhythm, baseline behaviour and direction. A wordmark set once and translated at the end reads as an afterthought in whichever language came second.

Clear space = X

X is the height of the symbol. Clear space is never a fixed millimetre value — stated in X, one rule scales from business card to billboard automatically.

Nothing enters this area — no other logo, no pattern, no text, no edge of the page.

Minimum size

If it dies at 16px, it is not finished. Test every concept at favicon size before it is chosen, not after. Thin strokes vanish and fine detail turns to mush — the fix is to thicken or simplify, and it is much cheaper before the mark is approved.

Colourways, ranked

A ranked chain, not a list — so an unanticipated situation still has an answer.

The single-colour version is mandatory, not a nicety. Engraving, embossing, foil, stamping and one-colour print all need it, and every one of those is a real surface for a physical brand.
