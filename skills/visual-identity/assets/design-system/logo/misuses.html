Misuses

Shown, not described. Eight wrong versions beside the right one communicate more than any paragraph — people copy pictures.

Give every rule its reason. A rule with a stated cause survives contact with a designer who wants to break it; a bare prohibition gets broken. Discord bans specific colour pairings and says why for each — "too vibrant when combined, creating an uncomfortable reading experience." That is why their rules hold.

Positioning

The mark sits in an upper corner or centred. It is never placed at the bottom of a layout.

A warning from a real guideline. The 2024 edition of one national brand book said in Arabic that the logo must never appear at the bottom, while its English page listed bottom as permitted. The 2026 edition corrected it. Bilingual rules must be cross-checked against each other, not translated and shipped — a designer reading one version will do what the other version forbids.
