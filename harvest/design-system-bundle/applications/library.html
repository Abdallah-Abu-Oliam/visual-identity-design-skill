⚠ What is not finished

Included deliberately. A book that hides its own loose ends teaches people to distrust the rest of it.

1 · The Pantone values are unverified. Every PMS reference in this book is a starting point produced on a screen. Pantone cannot be matched that way — the chips are printed ink under controlled light. Take a proof to a physical guide under D50 and pick by eye; the printer's choice replaces these values permanently.
2 · The public claim needs narrowing. "Nuclear does not cause cancer" is broader than what survives challenge. The defensible version: normal operation of nuclear power does not measurably raise cancer risk in surrounding populations, while high-dose radiation genuinely is carcinogenic — which is exactly why it is used to treat tumours. This is a tone-of-voice matter, not a visual one, but for a brand whose only asset is trust, one successful public correction costs more than the broad claim ever gained.
3 · The brand's photography does not exist. No free-licence library contains a volunteer sitting with a child in treatment — checked directly. Every image currently in circulation is a placeholder. This identity commits the organisation to commissioning its own, to the four categories and the dignity standard in Visual language.

Resolved, recorded so it is not reopened

The wheat separations. Originally painted white, which made the mark unusable reversed or on colour. Rebuilt by boolean subtraction in draw order so every gap is real negative space — cut only where a grain overlaps other ink, never where it sits on the background. A naive mask that cut them everywhere turned each grain into a hollow ring; that attempt was rendered, seen and reverted. All colourways and the one-colour master are now correct.
