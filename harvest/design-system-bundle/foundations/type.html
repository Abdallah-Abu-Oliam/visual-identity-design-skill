Foundations · four

A serif, in a category that is entirely sans.

Every pro-nuclear body audited uses a grotesque or geometric sans as its sole typographic class. Open Sans is the workhorse in five of seven childhood-cancer charities.

Why that matters more than taste. A warm serif is the cheapest differentiation available here — and it is a register move, not a hue move, which the research flagged as much harder to take back. Anyone can change a colour in an afternoon.

Warmth comes from the axes, not from a script face. Five of seven childhood-cancer charities reserve a handwritten script for exactly one emotional line — Schoolbell, Pacifico, Satisfy. It is the obvious warm move and it is taken. SOFT and WONK do the same job and nobody in either category is using them.
Use WONK sparingly. It is seasoning, not the dish. At display sizes it gives the informality the brief asks for; in running text it becomes a tic.

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next — body

La même science qui fait peur est celle qui soigne les enfants.

Stack"Atkinson Hyperlegible Next", "Segoe UI", system-ui, -apple-system, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
Axeswght 200–800
LicenceSIL Open Font License
The most directly derived decision in the identity. This face was drawn by the Braille Institute for low vision — letterforms deliberately shaped so characters cannot be confused with one another. The hardest constraint in this brief is that the material is read by unwell children, tired parents and clinical staff in a ward. A typeface exists that was designed for exactly that problem, and it is free. Choosing it is functional and a statement of who this organisation is.

Fallbacks are the specification

Not a footnote. A guideline without fallbacks dies the moment someone opens a document on a machine without the fonts. When a volunteer types a flyer on a ward computer, the fallback is what actually renders. Georgia carries the display because it is warm, widely installed and has a large x-height — it degrades gracefully rather than snapping to something cold.

Prohibitions

Each traces to a measured finding in the activist-register audit, not to taste.

No all-caps headlines, claims or accusations. Every campaign-register example reserved capitals for its most aggressive lines. Caps are permitted only for the small Label style — one short utilitarian word.
No ExtraBold as a default. Sortir du Nucléaire runs weight 800 as its body default; Greenpeace's webfont ships no light cut at all. A brand that can only shout has no indoor voice, and this one is an indoor voice.
No justified text. Ragged right always. Justification opens rivers at narrow measures, which is exactly where this brand's material lives.
No exclamation marks in display type. No letter-spacing on lowercase body text. No light text on Apricot.
