# Noyau — visual identity

A French nonprofit (association loi 1901) correcting the public belief that nuclear power
causes cancer. It does not argue. It goes into paediatric oncology wards, sits down, and
stays.

**The argument, in one line:** the same word names the nucleus of an atom and the core of a
family. What you were taught to fear is what treats your child.

---

## How to use this book

Every rule here carries its reason, in a `.why` block. That is structural, not decoration —
**a rule nobody can trace back to the brief gets overturned by the first person who dislikes
it.** If you disagree with a rule, read its reason first; several of them are derived from
measured competitor evidence or accessibility testing rather than taste, and those are not
local judgement calls.

**To rebrand or adjust:** edit `:root` in `styles.css`. Nothing else should need touching.
Keep `theme.json` in step so the machine-readable record does not drift from what the CSS
actually does.

## The five decisions everything else follows from

1. **The brand does not argue; it disarms.** Informal, playful, friendly, reserved, indirect —
   the owner set those together, deliberately. You cannot beat an entrenched fear by being
   direct at it.
2. **The identity is quiet and the call to action is the only loud thing in it.** That is why
   Apricot is the 10% accent and not the primary.
3. **Warmth without amber or yellow.** Both were occupied — amber by Réseau Sortir du
   Nucléaire, `#FFE600` by Orano. The name supplied the way out: a *noyau* is a fruit stone,
   and the fruit had a colour nobody held. **No blue anywhere.**
4. **The burst is taken; the glow is free.** Rays from a centre are the anti-nuclear
   movement's emblem, in continuous use since 1975. The trefoil is CRIIRAD's actual logo. The
   orbit-ellipse is used by three of five pro-nuclear bodies. None of them may appear here.
5. **Ownability comes from Le Grain, not from the logo.** A mark, a palette and a typeface are
   each one decision a competitor copies in an afternoon. A pattern built from the brand's own
   material cannot be taken without taking the brand.

## Three things are unfinished

Stated plainly, because a book that hides its loose ends teaches people to distrust the rest
of it. Full detail in `applications/library.html`.

1. **Pantone values are unverified** — matched on a screen, which is not valid. Confirm
   against a physical guide under D50 before ordering ink.
2. **The public claim needs narrowing** — "nuclear does not cause cancer" is broader than what
   survives challenge.
3. **The brand's photography does not exist** and must be commissioned. Every image currently
   in circulation is a placeholder.

**And one legal note:** the name has been **searched, not cleared**. No ® or ™ may be used. A
trademark attorney must confirm before registration or print.
