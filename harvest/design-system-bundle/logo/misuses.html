Eight wrong versions.

Shown rather than described, because people copy pictures. Each one carries the reason — a bare prohibition gets broken, a reasoned one survives.

Two of these are not generic don'ts. "Never on electric blue" and "never boxed" come from the competitor audit, not from taste — they name the two specific organisations Noyau is at risk of being confused with. A designer who has never met this brand needs to know that, and would not guess it.

Also prohibited, no picture needed

  • Recolouring individual parts. The mark is single-colour. Apricot blossoms on an ink tower is not a variant, it is a redraw.
  • Outlining. No strokes added around the silhouette.
  • Re-typesetting the wordmark. "Noyau" beside the symbol is set in Fraunces at the specified variation settings, not in whatever is installed.
  • Animating the mark's parts independently. If it moves, it moves as one object.
  • Using the full mark below 32px. Use the reduced variant. See Anatomy.
