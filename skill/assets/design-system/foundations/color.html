Where the accent came from. The category clustered in blue, green and yellow. Moving up the wheel toward red-orange produced #e85f1a. The palette is the competitor method's output, not a taste call.

60 / 30 / 10 is about area, not importance

The 60% is the colour the eye rests on, so it is usually the calmest. The 10% is where the eye goes. A palette fails most often by making the loudest colour dominant — exhausting to look at, and it leaves the accent nowhere to go.

Why four codes

CodeExists for
HEXWeb and digital handoff
RGBScreen — emitted light
CMYKProcess printing, four-colour
PMSSpot colour — exact, repeatable match
PMS is the one that gets dropped, and the one that matters most for physical goods. CMYK is mixed on press, so it drifts between runs, papers and suppliers. Pantone is pre-mixed ink — identical from every supplier, in every country. Packaging, signage and merchandise depend on it.

Contrast is not optional

Check every foreground and background pairing that will actually occur, and account for colour blindness. A scheme that carries meaning through red and green alone is invisible to a meaningful share of people. Harmony that cannot be read is a failed palette.

None of the three published guidelines studied for this kit — Coca-Cola, Discord or Saudi Founding Day — states a contrast ratio or mentions colour blindness. Requiring it here is an improvement on all of them, not an invention.
